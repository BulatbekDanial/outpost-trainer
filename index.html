        How to play

        1. Pick a site. The Moon's pole, the Moon's equator and Mars each break a different system.
        2. Pack the lander. 8,000 kg for power, life support, shielding and supplies. The engineering check shows if your base survives 60 days with no surprises.
        3. Run the outpost. Every 5 days choose what the crew does and which systems get power. React to dust storms, solar storms, sunsets and breakdowns.
        4. Debrief. See your grade and the real engineering lessons behind your choices.

        You lose if oxygen, water or food runs out. You score for science, crew morale, safe radiation levels and keeping reserves.
